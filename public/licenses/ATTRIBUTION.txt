https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/flygym

See NeuroMechFly-Apache-2.0.txt for the upstream license.

Modifications: original meshes and MuJoCo forward-kinematic poses exported to a compact binary/JSON format; coordinate normalization, orientation and browser playback. Movement is pose replay, not live MuJoCo dynamics or connectome emulation.
